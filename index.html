About Us

PT ANTRA NADI GLOBAL is an Indonesian-based company specializing in the production and export of premium quality coconut shell charcoal briquettes for shisha and BBQ. We are committed to delivering eco-friendly, smokeless, and long-lasting briquettes tailored to meet international standards.
